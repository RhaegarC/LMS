What you can assign

Assignments

Build each assignment from three kinds of instruction items.

📝

Text

Rich text — bold, lists, full instructions.

🎬

Video

A reference video for students to watch and mimic.

🎤

Voice

A short audio instruction you record.

Key features

  • Due date for each assignment (11:59 PM your time).
  • Draft mode — save quietly, work on it later. Nothing is visible to students until you publish.
  • Clone — duplicate a past assignment and reuse it with just a few tweaks.
  • Edit after publishing — tweak anything; students see the new version next time they look.
  • Assign to one class or several.
🎯

Built for variety

Mix text, video, and voice in a single assignment — so every task fits the lesson.

From students

Student submissions

Clear expectations, gentle deadlines, and no surprises for you.

  • ✓
    Every item answered — students respond to text, video, and voice items alike.
  • ✓
    Late work is accepted and clearly marked with a ⏰ Late badge — no penalty, just a heads-up for you.
  • ✓
    Students can edit their submission until you've published feedback. If a student resubmits after you started grading, your draft feedback is cleared and you're notified to grade again. No surprises.
Your feedback toolkit

Reviewing & grading ✍️

Grade quickly and kindly — right in the page.

🗂️

The pending queue

Every submission awaiting your feedback, oldest first. You only see your own classes.

▶️

The review player

Watch videos and listen to voice responses right in the page — with playback speed (0.5× → 2×) so long recordings don't eat your time.

⭐

Star rating per item

Rate each item 0.5 to 5.0 stars, half-stars allowed.

💬

Text feedback rich + quick

Rich text, plus quick comments — one tap, emoji included.

🎙️

Voice feedback personal touch

Record a personal message (up to 1 minute).

See the results

Reports & statistics 📊

Turn submissions into insight — for one assignment, one student, or the whole class.

📈

Assignment statistics

For any assignment, see at a glance:

  • Total submissions — who handed in, who didn't
  • On-time vs Late vs Not-submitted (counts + percentages)
  • Per-item average stars — spot which item students found hardest
🧒

Student progress card

A complete view of one student across assignments:

  • Every assignment + submission date (Late badge included)
  • Per-item star ratings & feedback summary
  • A trend — improving, steady, or declining
  • Filter by time range and by class
  • Export as PDF — perfect for parent–teacher meetings
🏫

Class engagement snapshot

A class-level overview for a specific assignment:

  • ✅ On-time / ⏰ Late / ❌ Not-submitted breakdown
  • Per-item star distribution — e.g. 5⭐ (6), 4⭐ (8), 3⭐ (2)
  • Visual bar chart of the submission breakdown
  • Export as PDF or CSV
